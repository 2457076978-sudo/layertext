# 龟兔再赛（评测集 eval01 · 自写 CC0）

## Chapter One

[P01] One spring morning, the animals of Maple Wood woke to a new notice on the old oak tree. Harry Hare had lost the great race a year before. He had never forgotten it. "The bridge was washed away by the rain last year," he said, "but this time nothing will stop me." Toby Tortoise only smiled and touched his little green cap.

[P02] The new race was planned by Owl herself. The start was fixed at the big stone. The finish was set by the river. The morning light, which was soft and gold, lay over the grass. The path that led up the hill was steep and full of little stones. "I will take the short road under the trees," said Harry, "because speed is my best friend."

[P03] At noon the race began, and Harry shot ahead like a yellow arrow. The animals who lived near the river all came to watch. He ran so fast that the leaves flew up behind him. Toby walked on, slow and steady, without one angry word. Near the river a fallen tree lay across the road. The small bridge had been closed by the keeper of the wood.

[P04] Harry laughed when he passed the halfway stone. "Good luck, my slow friend!" he called over his shoulder. Then he stopped to rest, and the warm sun made him sleepy. When Harry woke, Toby had passed the fallen tree.

[P05] The whole wood knew the end of the story by now, but nobody said it out loud. Toby won the race, and the second place was taken by a very tired hare. When the shadows were long, Harry was still running. The lesson was remembered by every young animal in Maple Wood, and even the fastest runner now stopped to look at the world around him.
