# 科学展（评测集 eval02 · 自写 CC0）

## Chapter One

[P01] Last Friday our school held its big science fair in the hall. Every class was asked to bring one project, and the best one would win a new set of books. My friend Lin and I had worked on a small robot for three whole weeks. We had built it out of an old toy car and some cheap parts from a night market.

[P02] Our robot was called Little Star, and it could find a black line on white paper. The little motor was joined to the wheels with thin wire, and a small battery gave power to the whole system. A light that flashed red and green sat on its head like a small hat. Lin, who was better at drawing than me, painted a silver star on its back.

[P03] On the morning of the fair, the hall was filled with noisy students and bright posters. A robot that moved across the table by itself won the loudest cheers from all the younger students near the door. Our teacher Chen walked around with a notebook and asked quiet questions. "Your plan was approved by the office this morning," he told us with a warm smile.

[P04] When the judges came to our table, my hands were shaking and my heart was beating fast. Little Star followed the black line perfectly for two minutes. Then she turned left at a corner and ran straight into a box of paper cups. The cups flew everywhere, and everybody laughed, but the judges were not angry at all.

[P05] In the end we were given a small prize for the most honest project. Before the fair I had never spoken to so many people in one day. By noon the judges had finished half of the tables. Lin said that the best part was not the prize but the fun of building something together. We are already thinking about next year, when our school will hold the fair again.
