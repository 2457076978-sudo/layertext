# 灯塔（评测集 eval03 · 自写 CC0）

## Chapter One

[P01] The old lighthouse stood alone on the grey rock at the north end of the island. It was built by Ben's grandfather more than sixty years ago. Its white walls had been washed by ten thousand storms. Every night Ben climbed the cold stone steps and lit the great lamp at the top.

[P02] Ben's father, who kept the light before him, had gone to the town for the winter. The boy lived there with a black cat and a radio that talked to him from across the sea. Never had Ben seen such a dark sea. In his letters, his mother often asked him to come home before Christmas.

[P03] One evening a thick fog came in from the sea, and the light went out. Ben, who knew every step of the old stair, climbed up with a small lantern. The lamp, which had never failed his family, was dry and cold. He found the trouble at once: sea water had run into the oil box during the storm.

[P04] Ben worked all night, and by morning the great light was burning again. A ship that carried tea and paper had passed the island safely at two in the morning. When the captain learned about the boy in the tower, he sent a long letter of thanks to the island school. The letter was given to the island school, and everybody clapped for the young keeper at the morning meeting.

[P05] In spring the tide brings shell fish to the rocks below the tower, and white gulls fish there all morning. Ben's grandfather, who loved this wild place more than any city, always said that a keeper's life was the best life in the world.
