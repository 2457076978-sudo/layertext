# QC 规则覆盖测试文本（torture test）

> 自写测试文本（CC0）。刻意覆盖全部防坑规则：倒装过去完成、had+规则/不规则分词、
> 直接引语豁免、假阳性豁免（red/mixed/exhausted/interested）、that 三类豁免、
> who/which 定从、不规则分词被动、"逗号+分词+by"后置定语、词形还原、待定词命中、
> 超 20 词长句、词句卡并入已知。

## Chapter One

[P01] Never had I seen such a tall windmill before. I had seen one only in a picture book. That morning, the two boys had eaten all the bread before sunrise. He said that he was still hungry. The horse that pulled the cart was old and thin, and it wore a heavy leather harness.

[P02] The old mill, built by my grandfather, still turned slowly in the wind. The girl who lived next door kept two white rabbits. The story which he told us was funny and a little sad. The geese and the sheep were driven across the road. The walls are painted every year.

[P03] He said, "The bridge was destroyed by the storm." Then he added, "The road was covered with wet snow." Nobody believed him at first. Later the fence was painted green. The barn was red. She was exhausted. The team was mixed. He had mixed the two kinds of seed. The boys were interested in the plan.

[P04] Hardly had she spoken when the bell rang. No sooner had he eaten than the rain stopped. They carried the heavy boxes up the narrow stairs. The children studied hard for the test on Friday. His feet stopped hurting after the medicine. The fire went out in the cold night air.

[P05] He led a quiet life, lived by the river and the small wood. The old man sang a song about the good old days while the fat cat slept in the sun beside him and the wind turned the great slow arms of the mill above the quiet green meadow full of flowers and happy young sheep all summer long.
