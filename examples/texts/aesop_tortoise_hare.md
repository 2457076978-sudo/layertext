# 龟兔赛跑（伊索寓言 · 自写英文复述 · CC0）

## Chapter One

[P01] Once there was a hare who laughed at a slow tortoise. "You walk like an old grandmother," he said. The tortoise smiled and said, "Let us run a race to the old oak tree."

[P02] The race, watched by all the animals of the field, began at noon the next day. The hare, which always ran very fast, was sure that he would win. He ran far ahead, and then he stopped to sleep under a cool green tree, because the day was warm and he felt tired.

[P03] While the hare slept, the tortoise kept walking, step by step, without stopping. When the hare woke up, the sun had already gone down. He ran as fast as he could, but the tortoise had reached the oak tree first.

[P04] The animals cheered, and the hare learned an important lesson that day: slow and steady can win the race. The tortoise, tired but happy, went home with his head high.
