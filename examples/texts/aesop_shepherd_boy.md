# 狼来了（伊索寓言 · 自写英文复述 · CC0）

## Chapter One

[P01] A shepherd boy looked after his sheep near a small village. The village was at the foot of a green mountain, and the people there were kind but busy. The boy often felt bored, because the days were long and quiet.

[P02] One day he ran to the village and shouted, "Wolf! Wolf! A wolf is eating my sheep!" The farmers dropped their tools and ran up the mountain, but they found no wolf. The boy laughed at them, and they went back to their work with angry faces.

[P03] He played the same trick two more times. On the third day, a real wolf came out of the woods. The boy cried for help, but nobody believed him, because he had told lies so many times before. The wolf ate many of the sheep, and the boy learned that honesty matters.
