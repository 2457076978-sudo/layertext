# 社团招新（校园故事 · 自写 · CC0）

## Chapter One

[P01] Lin Tao and his cousin Wang Fang were new students at Green Field School. The school stood at the foot of a hill, and its library, built in 1958, was the oldest building in town. On the first morning, the head teacher showed them around the campus.

[P02] "There are more than thirty clubs here," said Mr. Chen, the teacher who led the art club. "You can join any club you like, but choose carefully, because each student can only join two."

[P03] Lin Tao was interested in science, so he visited the science room first. The room was full of old machines, glass bottles and maps of the stars. A tall boy was repairing a model plane that had flown across the playground the week before.

[P04] Wang Fang liked music. She had learned the piano for six years before her family moved to this city. In the music room, a girl was playing a piece that her grandmother had taught her. The music was so beautiful that everybody stopped to listen.

[P05] In the end, Lin Tao chose the science club, and Wang Fang joined the music club. They agreed that the first week at Green Field School was the best week of their lives. That evening, they wrote a long letter to their parents about their new school, their new teachers and their new friends.
